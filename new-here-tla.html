What you get
A vote on which liquidity Terra DeFi rewards. Direct emissions to your own LPs (redeem as LUNA, or amplify and grow the LP), or place VP where the bribes are and get paid weekly in the bribe token. Your LST keeps earning chain rewards underneath.
What you give up
Chain governance: your stake weight sits with the validators Eris chooses, and that weight is concentrated. Liquidity: 1 week to 2 years locked — the only early exit is selling the lock at a discount. Attention: VP is fixed at lock time; keep adding or extending or it drifts down.

What you get
The highest LST growth on offer: Votion's optimizer votes the vault's VP wherever bribes and emissions pay best (re-optimizing every ~15 minutes until the cast deadline), claims, and compounds into the vault's exchange rate. You hold a receipt token; its value in LST only goes up.
What you give up
Your TLA voice — Votion votes, not you. Liquidity — the vault has its own lock schedule on top of the LST, and there is no Atrium/Boost market to sell out of it early. And the 7-day number moves in steps: rewards land at each epoch, so short windows mislead why?
