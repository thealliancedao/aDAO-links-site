Optimizer Watch

A vote optimizer decides where a large block of Alliance votes goes each week. This page shows that decision in full: where the votes go and why, which bribed pools get none and why, every pool on the list, and the round's bribes as they change.

This is aDAO's page, not Votion's, and aDAO is not affiliated with Votion or Eris. It is an example of what an optimizer's own page could show, built only from public data: the plan Votion publishes (read hourly), the bribe contract on chain (read live in your browser) and aDAO's public records. Where the public data gives no reason, the page says so and does not guess.
