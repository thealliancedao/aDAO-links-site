About Treasury Tracking

Balances are live from chain. History is the org dao-dashboard daily archive (one point per day since 2026-08-12; older epoch files lived in a retired repo and are not read). Every day Eris's bot claims the NFT contract's alliance rewards, bonds them to ampLUNA and sends 10% to this treasury — the Protocol Income block above is that ledger, one row per claim tx. Amount charts move in straight, steady lines because that daily take is the only regular inflow; proposals that move tokens appear as gold points on the chart and in the list on the right. Click any token row to chart it.
