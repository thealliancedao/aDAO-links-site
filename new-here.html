Chapter 1

The NFT that is not a JPEG

Most NFTs are a picture and a promise. The Alliance DAO NFT is a picture and a stake: the collection's smart contract holds a staked asset called Ally, and Terra's governance voted to reward that stake alongside ordinary LUNA staking.

That vote was Terra governance proposal #4801, passed 2024-02-23. It gave Ally a reward weight of 0.008 in the chain's Alliance module — a fixed, pinned share of the LUNA staking rewards the chain pays out. The community pushed the weight up from the 0.003 in the forum draft before it went on chain.

So the collection's rewards don't come from a team, a treasury drip or a token they printed. They come from the chain itself, in LUNA, every day — the same source that pays validators and delegators. That is the whole thesis, and it is the part nobody else can copy without a governance vote of their own.

The honest caveat, which this site always shows: the rewards are a grant from Terra governance and a future proposal could change or remove them. Everything here is derived from the recorded history, not a promise.

Chapter 2

How the backing grows — four steps, one transaction, every day

You don't have to understand staking to follow this. Read it top to bottom once and you'll recognise every number on the home page.

1Claim

Around 00:50 UTC, a bot calls the NFT contract and collects all the LUNA rewards the chain has accrued to Ally since yesterday.

2Bond to ampLUNA

In the same transaction, every LUNA is staked again through Eris into ampLUNA — LUNA that keeps earning staking yield by itself. The backing isn't idle coins; it is staked coins.

390% to holders

Nine-tenths stays in the NFT contract as the backing, shared equally by every unbroken NFT.

410% to the DAO

One-tenth goes to the DAO wallet as operating funds — the money that builds this site and the DAO's TLA positions (chapter 5).

Chapter 3

Breaking: cashing out your share, once

At any time, a holder can break an NFT. The contract pays that NFT's share of the backing out to the holder — one share of the whole pool — and that NFT stops accruing. It can be done once per NFT.

You keep the NFT. It still trades, it still carries its voting power in the DAO, it is still your art. What it gives up is the daily backing.

And here is the part people miss: the daily claim does not shrink when NFTs break. The same 90% is divided among fewer NFTs — so every unbroken NFT's daily gain rises as others break. The Ally page calls this "last NFT standing" and projects it from the recorded series.

Chapter 5

The DAO: what we do with it

Who decides

Holders stake their NFTs in DAODAO and vote — one staked NFT, one vote, broken or not. Every proposal, every vote and every voter is on the DAO page, with a quick audit of what each proposal actually does on chain.

Where the treasury works

The DAO's 10% goes into the Terra Liquidity Alliance: liquidity positions that earn LUNA emissions, and two max-duration locks that give the DAO votes over where those emissions flow. The treasury is worth — today, and that strip on the home page is the four parts of it.

Why the unminted matter

— NFTs of the 10,000 were never sold. The DAO holds them to build alliances — with other collections, other chains, other DAOs — and each one it pairs is a new holder pulled into Terra DeFi. That is the mission in one sentence: get more people using Terra, by making it worth their while.

What this site is for

TLA Stats exists so that a member — or anyone — can see the whole system honestly: what the DAO owns, where the emissions go, who holds the votes, which pools are worth a vote and why, what the biggest briber says versus what it does, and whether the NFT's backing did today what it did yesterday. Every number links to the on-chain product it came from, every rule the DAO votes by is published, and the help bot has read the same documents you can.

Chapter 6

Terra DeFi in plain words — the eight things to know

Staking. Locking LUNA to help secure the chain; the chain pays you a share of new LUNA for it. Ally is staked this way, which is why the NFTs earn.
ampLUNA, arbLUNA, bLUNA (liquid staking tokens). A receipt for staked LUNA that you can still trade or use. It quietly gains value against LUNA as the staking rewards compound — ampLUNA is worth — LUNA today. The NFT backing is held as ampLUNA for exactly this reason.
A liquidity pool (LP). Two tokens deposited together so people can trade between them. Depositors earn trading fees — and on Terra, LUNA emissions steered by TLA.
The Terra Liquidity Alliance (TLA). Terra redirects part of its staking emissions to liquidity pools instead of printing a new token. Which pools get it is decided by votes — every week, in four "buckets" (stable, project, bluechip, single).
Voting power (VP). Lock a liquid staking token for up to a year and you get votes in TLA. The DAO's two locks are its VP. Votes point emissions at pools.
Bribes. Anyone can put a pot on a pool to attract votes; voters who point there share the pot. About a quarter of all TLA votes belong to Votion, which moves mechanically to whatever pays best — this site reproduces its optimizer so you can see what a pot would move.
A DAO. A treasury governed by token or NFT holders through proposals executed by a smart contract. Alliance DAO is one; so are Lion DAO and PixelLions, our allies.
Take rate vs treasury cut. Two different 10%s that share a name. TLA's take rate is charged on amplified LP rewards; the NFT's 10% is the DAO's share of the daily claim. Never confuse them — this site never says "take rate" unqualified.
