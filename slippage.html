Arb Radar — where pools disagree on price

The same pair trading at different prices in two pools is a round-trip profit — until someone takes it. how it's estimated

The model: standard (xyk) pools only — a pool's reserve ratio is its price, so two xyk pools holding the same pair at different ratios disagree, and buying in the cheaper one while selling in the richer one profits by the spread minus both legs' price impact and swap fees (0.3% × 2 assumed). Best size and profit come from the exact xyk optimum on live reserves. Concentrated and stable pools are excluded on purpose: they hold imbalanced reserves by design, which would read as fake spreads. Honest caveats: bots race visible spreads and may beat you in the same block; SkeletonSwap reserves are unverified upstream; this ignores gas (tiny on Terra) and multi-hop alternatives. A planning signal, not a quote.
