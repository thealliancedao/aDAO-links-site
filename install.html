Get the Ally app

No app store. It installs from this page in about thirty seconds, updates itself, and opens full-screen on your phone.

Install now

Your browser can install it directly.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open thealliancedao.com/app.html in Safari (Chrome and Firefox on iOS work too on iOS 16.4 or newer — same steps, the Share button is in their menu).
  2. Tap Share at the bottom of the screen (top-right on iPad).
  3. Scroll the sheet and tap Add to Home Screen .
  4. Tap Add. The Ally icon appears on your home screen.

Don't see "Add to Home Screen"? You're probably inside another app's browser (Telegram, X, Discord). Tap the ··· or there and choose Open in Safari first.

Android

  1. Open thealliancedao.com/app.html in Chrome.
  2. If a bar or banner offers Install or Add Ally to Home screen, tap it — done.
  3. Otherwise tap the menu ⋮ (top-right) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then Install.
Samsung Internet, Firefox, Edge

Samsung Internet: menu ≡ → Add page to → Home screen.
Firefox: menu ⋮ → Install (or Add to Home screen).
Edge: menu ··· → Add to phone.

Inside Telegram or X? Tap ⋮ → Open in Chrome first.

Desktop

The app is built for phones — on a computer, the full site is the better experience. If you still want it as a window: in Chrome or Edge, open app.html and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (or menu → Install Ally).

To get it on your phone, open thealliancedao.com/app.html there — or scan this page's address from your phone's camera.

Which icon you get

Installing from app.html (or this page) gives you the Ally icon that opens the app. Installing from a page of the site gives you an icon labelled for that page — Home, NFT, TLA, DAO or Help — that opens the app on that tab. Pin the two or three you use.

Set it up your way

Your walletOpen Me and pick your name, paste your address, or type its last 4 characters. It's remembered on that device. Change or Clear any time.
Your five tabsTap ⚙ and choose which five of Feed · Me · Market · TLA · DAO · NFT · Help sit on the bar.
The badgeThe number on the icon is proposals posted since you last looked. In ⚙ choose which DAO it counts — all of them, or just yours.
The FeedEvery card shows what changed since your last open — floor, listings, sales, TLA liquidity, LUNA, the epoch. Tap Mark all as seen when you're caught up.
Where pages openIn ⚙ set what NFT Explorer opens on (Explorer or Analytics), what TLA Stats opens on (Overview, Pools, LP Grades, TLA), and your default marketplace collection.
UpdatesAutomatic. Every open loads the latest version — nothing to reinstall. Only a changed icon needs a remove-and-re-add.

Privacy: nothing leaves your phone. Your wallet choice and settings are stored on the device only; the app reads the same public on-chain data as the site.

Troubleshooting

  • The icon badge never shows on iPhone. iOS shows a badge only once the app has notification permission — we don't ask for that yet, so on iPhone look for the red count on the Feed tab instead. Android shows both.
  • It asked for my wallet again after installing. The installed app has its own storage separate from the browser — pick it once more and it sticks.
  • Something looks stale. Tap at the top. The app never caches data; if a number is old, the capture behind it is — the site's System Health page says which.
