How to read a change

Passed proposal

The change is a DAO proposal being executed. The vote is shown and linked.

Sent directly

An account sent the change itself; no proposal was executed in that transaction. Some powers belong to an account (the admin that can upgrade a contract), so this is the usual route for those. A change that leaves the route every earlier change of its kind took is marked.

Seen in a state read

The chain gave one value at one read and another at the next. It shows before and after — not who sent it.

Needs a look

Most changes are ordinary: a passed proposal being executed, or the holder of a key doing what that key has always been used for. A change is marked when the record itself shows one of four things:

  • Control moved from a DAO to an account. A role or an admin key that took a passed vote to use can now be used by whoever holds one account’s key.
  • Not the route it took before. Every earlier change of that kind to that contract came through a passed proposal; this one was sent directly.
  • A vote on it was open, or did not pass. A proposal asked for the same kind of change on the same contract, and the change was sent directly anyway.
  • No transaction explains it. A state read found a difference and the record, complete for that time, holds no transaction for it.

The mark is not a verdict. It says where a change left the usual route, with the reason beside it, so that anyone can check it.

This page states what the chain shows: what changed, when, who sent it and how. It does not say why.
