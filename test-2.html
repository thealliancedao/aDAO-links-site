Vote Market

Bribes pay voters. A pool's pot is split between everyone voting it: your cut = pot × your votes ÷ all its votes. A pool must hold 1% of its bucket's votes to pay out and earn emissions.
Votion follows the money. Its ~8.6M VP (in every bucket) goes wherever pot ÷ votes is best. Add a bribe and it moves votes in — from the pools paying least. We re-run its maths exactly.
Votes set next week's emissions. Each bucket's weekly LUNA is split by vote share among pools over the 1% line, so votes → emissions → APR for LPs.
It's a forecast. Everything uses this round's pots and votes right now. Other voters and bribers can change it before the round closes, and Votion's own solver lands a few % short of the best split — read the direction and size, not the decimals.
