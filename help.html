Frequently asked

Why is a column or tile blank instead of showing a number?
Deliberate policy, not a bug: the site never invents a number it can't back. If a pool shows a blank Efficiency, there is no trustworthy volume source for it (SkeletonSwap publishes none). If a stat says "no data", the capture that would feed it doesn't exist yet or its source went dark. Everywhere possible the blank carries a tooltip saying which. A blank is the honest answer; a made-up number would just be a lie with confidence.
The Live Activity feed doesn't show something I did today.
Activity comes from the nightly capture rollup (~00:00 UTC). Actions land in the feed the day after they happen. This is stated on the card ("updates nightly") — it's a data-pipeline cadence, not a missed event.
What do the LP grades actually mean?
A grade scores how much a vote for that pool strengthens the TLA system — trading quality (depth, utilization, trend, exit slippage) times asset durability (token verification, acquisition risk, take-rate flow). It is NOT deposit advice and NOT a payout ranking (that's the Bounty Board). "Provisional" means fewer than four complete epochs of agreeing data — the grade exists but hasn't earned full confidence yet. New pools get a "new" state, not an F: absence of history isn't evidence of weakness. The full rubric, weights included, is public in the grading config — the tooltips on the Grades tab echo the exact weights used.
The footer dot is amber/red — is the site broken?
The dot answers one question: is the data flowing on schedule? Amber = a scheduled job is running late; red = something is genuinely stale. Click the dot — it opens the System Health page listing every job, its age, and which producer to look at. One important rule: a product reporting a finding (e.g. system-health flagging an invariant) is that job working — findings never turn the dot red.
Two pages show slightly different VP totals — which is right?
Both, usually — they answer different questions. "Total TLA VP" uses the max-bucket convention (matches Eris UI); pool-summed VP counts each voter once per bucket (up to 4×) and is only used where per-pool context needs it, always labeled. A lock's own VP = boosted underlying + fixed amount (the canonical definition since the 2026-07 VP spec). If you find a genuinely inconsistent pair, that's exactly what the report form is for.
Where does the data come from? Can I verify it?
Every number traces to public chain data captured by open cron jobs into a public GitHub repo (thealliancedao/tla-core) — the same files this site fetches are the files you can read. The Transparency Hub lists every producer, its schedule, and its heartbeat. Nothing is hand-entered; where attribution isn't certain, wallets stay unnamed by rule.
Why does the Bribe Runway matter to me as a depositor?
When a pool's bribe pot empties, Votion re-optimizes its votes away and vote weight follows — the pool's rewards drop. The runway shows how many epochs of funding each pot has left, so you can adjust LP positions before the cliff instead of after. Linear-distribution bribes taper, so per-epoch amounts can shrink before the stated end date.
Is any of this financial advice?
No. The site reports measured facts about the TLA system — depths, votes, grades, runways — so you can make your own decisions. Grades score system benefit, not your returns. Nothing here recommends buying, selling, or holding anything.
Deeper reading: the Transparency Hub carries the full documentation library — what each data product is, the TLA mechanics primer, and the honesty rules this site is built on.
